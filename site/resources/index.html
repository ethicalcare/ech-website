Home-care guides and answers

Clear answers for the decisions families do not expect to make.

These guides explain the questions that often arise when care at home begins to change: what support may help, what care can cost, how public and private options differ, and what to plan for after a hospital stay.

When the situation is still taking shape

Begin with the life already happening at home.

  • What has changed, and when does the day feel hardest?
  • What matters most to the person receiving care?
  • Which family, public or private support is already in place?
  • What is the family carrying now, and what no longer feels sustainable?
  • A sudden or severe change needs urgent medical attention.
