The short answer

What happens after a family contacts Ethical Care?

A first conversation focuses on what the person wants, what has changed, which support is already in place, where care is needed and how soon help may be required. Ethical Care can then explain the next appropriate step and confirm current availability.

Helpful to share

Keep the first message focused.

  • The general location where care is needed
  • What has changed and what the person wants
  • Which family, public or private support is already involved
  • The likely days, times or discharge date
  • Whether help may be needed soon

For urgent help

This contact page is not emergency care.

  • Call 911 for an emergency
  • Contact the appropriate clinician or health service for an urgent health change
  • Do not send medication lists, diagnoses or medical records through an unsecured first message
  • Ask how sensitive information should be shared before sending it
