What matters in the home

Care that is both human and dependable.

  • Respect the person’s choices, privacy and home
  • Follow the individual care approach and ask when something is unclear
  • Notice changes and communicate them promptly
  • Arrive reliably and support clear handoffs
  • Work respectfully with families, nurses and other professionals
  • Be honest about qualifications and limits
