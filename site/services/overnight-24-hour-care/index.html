Overnight & 24-Hour Care

Could this help your family?

This care may be worth considering when…

  • A person who needs support or supervision during the night
  • A family caregiver who can no longer provide overnight coverage
  • Someone with dementia, palliative or higher-support needs
  • A family considering continuous rotating care at home

What support can look like

Care shaped around the person’s day.

  • Awake overnight care when active support is expected
  • Sleeping overnight or live-in arrangements when appropriate
  • Rotating 24-hour caregiver shifts with defined handoffs
  • Overnight dementia, respite or comfort-focused support when it fits the person’s needs
  • A clear plan for backup and what to do if needs change

How the nurse-led approach supports this care

Clear roles help the person, family and care team work together.

A nurse may help clarify health concerns or care instructions that affect the night, but most overnight support is not automatically nursing care. Your family should know who will be in the home, whether that person remains awake and which tasks require a nurse.

Clear expectations

What your family should know before deciding.

  • Ask whether “24-hour” refers to rotating care in the home, telephone availability or something else.
  • Sleeping or live-in arrangements need a suitable place to rest and clear limits on interruptions.
  • Visit length, minimum scheduling, staffing and backup should be confirmed for your family’s location.

A simple way to begin

Your family should know what happens next.

  1. Tell us what is happening

    Share what the person wants, what feels harder and when help may be needed.

  2. Talk through the options

    We will explain which kinds of support may be worth considering.

  3. Know who will do what

    Your family will know what a caregiver, nurse or other professional would provide.

  4. Stay in touch as needs change

    Let us know when something changes so the support can be looked at again.

Clear answers

Questions about overnight and 24-hour care

What is awake overnight care?

The caregiver remains awake because assistance or observation may be needed during the night. Before care begins, your family should understand the usual tasks, frequency of help and what happens if needs increase.

Is live-in care the same as 24-hour care?

No. Live-in or sleeping arrangements include defined rest periods; continuous 24-hour care generally uses rotating workers.

How is cost determined?

Cost depends on whether care is awake, sleeping or continuous, as well as the hours, level of support, staffing and schedule. Ask for a written explanation of what the arrangement includes and any minimum hours.
