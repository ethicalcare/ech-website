What kinds of home care can Ethical Care help with?

Ethical Care offers companionship and personal care, dementia support, respite for family caregivers, help after a hospital stay, nurse-led and clinical care, overnight support, palliative care and coordinated rehabilitation. What is appropriate depends on the person’s needs, location, timing and the qualifications required.

Clear care roles

Your family should know who is helping and why.

Some needs can be supported by a caregiver. Others need care from a registered nurse or another health professional. We explain those differences so your family can decide with clearer expectations.

  • Caregivers can help with agreed daily routines, personal care and household tasks based on their training.
  • Qualified nurses provide health assessments and nursing treatments when those services are needed.
  • Rehabilitation professionals assess and guide recovery needs within their area of expertise.
  • For an urgent medical concern or emergency, contact the appropriate clinical service or call 911.
