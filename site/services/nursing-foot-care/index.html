Could this help your family?

This care may be worth considering when…

  • Someone unable to manage routine foot or nail care safely
  • A person with health factors that require nursing assessment
  • A family seeking an in-home nursing foot-care service
  • Someone whose foot concern may need another clinician

What support can look like

Care shaped around the person’s day.

  • Nail and skin care that fits the nurse’s foot-care training
  • Risk screening and observation within the nurse’s qualifications
  • Prevention education and home-care guidance
  • Equipment and infection-control practices
  • Referral when a concern needs another clinician

Nursing Foot Care

How the nurse-led approach supports this care

The nursing role is explained before care begins.

This service is provided by a nurse with appropriate foot-care education and competence. The nurse can explain what falls within nursing foot care and when a concern needs a physician, podiatrist or another clinician.

Clear expectations

What your family should know before deciding.

  • Ask about the nurse’s qualifications, equipment cleaning and infection-control practices.
  • Not every foot concern is appropriate for nursing foot care.
  • Nursing foot care cannot promise an outcome or replace medical treatment for a foot condition.

A simple way to begin

Your family should know what happens next.

  1. Tell us what is happening

    Share what the person wants, what feels harder and when help may be needed.

  2. Talk through the options

    We will explain which kinds of support may be worth considering.

  4. Stay in touch as needs change

Clear answers

Questions about nursing foot care

Who provides nursing foot care?

A nurse with the education and competence required for the foot-care services being offered should provide the visit. Ask which nursing designation and foot-care training apply.

Is this the same as podiatry?

No. Nursing foot care and podiatry are different services. The nurse will explain what can be provided during the visit and when a physician, podiatrist or another clinician should be involved.

Is the service available in every area?

Availability depends on where care is needed, the schedule and whether a qualified nurse is currently available. Contact Ethical Care with your location to confirm.
