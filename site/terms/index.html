What this website does not do.

The short answer

Does using this website create a care relationship?

No. Reading the website or sending a message does not confirm service, create a clinical relationship or guarantee availability. Care begins only after Ethical Care and the family complete the appropriate conversation, assessment and agreement.

Website content is provided for general information about home care and related questions. It does not diagnose, treat or provide advice for a specific person. Always seek advice from an appropriate healthcare professional about a person’s health, medications, symptoms or treatment.
