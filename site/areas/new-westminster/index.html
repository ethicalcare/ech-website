The short answer

Can Ethical Care help a family in New Westminster?

Yes. New Westminster is part of Ethical Care’s Lower Mainland mobile service area. The team will first understand what support the person needs and when help may need to begin, then confirm the schedule and appropriate care team before your family relies on a start date.

Planning care locally

Support for daily life at home and the transition back after hospital.

Families may be balancing discharge instructions, mobility changes, personal care and the limits of what a spouse or adult child can manage. A clear plan explains which support can happen at home and who will provide each part.

  • A return home from Royal Columbian Hospital needs practical preparation
  • Apartment access or elevators affect how visits are planned
  • A primary caregiver needs scheduled respite before becoming overwhelmed
  • Nursing and daily-living support need clearly separated responsibilities

Clear answers

Questions about home care in New Westminster.

Can Ethical Care help after discharge from Royal Columbian Hospital?

It may be possible. Ethical Care is not affiliated with Royal Columbian Hospital. Contact the team before discharge when you can, and share the expected date, New Westminster address and support described in the discharge instructions.

What apartment or building details should our family share?

Entry instructions, elevators, parking, mobility equipment and any timing restrictions can affect the visit plan. Sharing those practical details early can prevent confusion on the first day home.

What if a spouse can no longer manage the same level of care?

Tell the team which parts of the day have become difficult and what the spouse or family can still manage safely. Respite, personal care, companion support or nursing may each have a different role.

How will we know whether a caregiver or nurse is needed?

A caregiver may help with agreed daily routines and personal support. Health assessment and clinical treatments require an appropriately qualified nurse. Ethical Care should explain who would provide each part before care begins.
