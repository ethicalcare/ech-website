About Ethical Care at Home

Care at home should feel personal and clear.

Ethical Care at Home is a Coquitlam-based home-care organization supporting Lower Mainland families. The approach brings everyday support, nursing care and coordinated professional help together around the person’s goals, routines and changing needs.

The short answer

What is nurse-led home care?

Nurse-led home care means a registered nurse can help the family understand health and day-to-day needs, ask the right clinical questions and clarify who should provide each part of care. It does not mean a nurse provides every visit.

What guides the conversation

Begin with the person, not a service list.

A useful plan considers what the person wants to keep doing, where help is needed, who is already involved and what the family can realistically manage.

  • Protect dignity, choice and familiar routines
  • Match the right kind of support to each need
  • Explain who will provide each part of care
  • Keep families informed with the person’s consent
  • Look at the care approach again when needs change

What families should expect

Clear roles and honest next steps.

  • A conversation about the whole situation
  • A plain explanation of care options
  • Clarity about caregiver, nursing and professional roles
  • Current availability confirmed before a start date
  • Honesty when another provider or service is more appropriate
